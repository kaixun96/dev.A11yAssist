The current [Common / Fluent / SharePoint KB](../accessibility-kb/README.md)
owns the content, its source bindings and ongoing review. Content packages are
at `0.1.1`; the independently versioned service is at `0.1.0`.

AgentOW and its archive are not operational, build, installation or source-map
dependencies of the knowledge service or current KB maintenance. No per-entry
AgentOW mapping is required, and the archive is not an active MCP source catalog.

Current entries remain draft. Historical sources and attribution do not establish
official approval, installed-version validity, redistribution permission or
observed conformance. Qualification follows the
[contribution policy](../accessibility-kb/governance/contribution.md).

This filename is retained for link compatibility. For current architecture and
authoring, see the [technical design](TECH-DESIGN.md).
